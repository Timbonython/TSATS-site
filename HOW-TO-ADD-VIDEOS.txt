THE SUN AND THE SKY — website
==============================

To view the site:
  Double-click  index.html  (it opens in your browser).

To add your YouTube links:
  1. Open  index.html  in any text editor (TextEdit is fine).
  2. Near the bottom, find the block titled
        CONTENT CONFIG — EDIT YOUR YOUTUBE LINKS HERE
  3. There are two lists:
        VIDEOS  -> the 3 films (first one is the big feature)
        TRACKS  -> up to 9 audio tracks
  4. For each one, paste your link between the quotes after  youtube:
        youtube:"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XXXXXXXXXXX"
     A bare ID also works:
        youtube:"XXXXXXXXXXX"
     Leave it empty ( youtube:"" ) to keep the "coming soon" placeholder.
  5. While you're there you can rename the titles (title:"...").
  6. Save, refresh the browser. Done.

Folder layout:
  index.html            the website
  assets/img            hero, logo, favicon
  assets/posters        the 6 gig posters
  assets/gallery        the 12 photos

To put it online later: upload the whole "TSATS Site" folder to any
web host (Netlify, GitHub Pages, your own server). No build step needed.
